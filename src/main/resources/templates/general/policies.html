Eligibility Rules

Who Can Apply for Leave?

Review the eligibility criteria and important conditions before applying for leave.

  • Leave benefits are available only to eligible employees.
  • Leave requests must be submitted through the Leave Management System.
  • Employees must have sufficient leave balance before applying.
  • Planned leave should be applied for at least 2 working days in advance.
  • Emergency leave should be reported to the reporting manager immediately.
  • Medical certificates may be required for extended sick leave.
  • Half-day leave can be requested where permitted by company policy.
  • Leave approval depends on manager approval and business requirements.
  • Unauthorized absence may result in Leave Without Pay (LWP).
  • Employees should verify company holidays before planning leave.

Policy Guidelines

Important Leave Guidelines

Please review the following guidelines before applying for leave.

  • Apply for planned leave at least 2 working days in advance.
  • All leave requests must be submitted through the Leave Management System.
  • Ensure sufficient leave balance before submitting a request.
  • Sick leave extending beyond company policy may require a medical certificate.
  • Inform your reporting manager immediately in case of emergency leave.
  • Leave is considered approved only after manager and HR authorization.
  • Leave dates can be modified or cancelled before approval, subject to policy.
  • Unauthorized absence may result in Leave Without Pay (LWP) or disciplinary action.
  • Public holidays and weekly offs are governed by the official company calendar.
  • Employees are responsible for tracking leave balance and application status.
