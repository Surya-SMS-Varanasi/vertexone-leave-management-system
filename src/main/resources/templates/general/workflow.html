Leave Approval Work-flow

Follow every stage of the leave approval process from application to final confirmation.

Step 1
Employee Applies

Employee fills the leave application,
selects leave type and submits the request.

Step 2
Manager Reviews

Reporting Manager reviews the leave request
and verifies eligibility.

Step 3
HR Verification

HR Verifies leave as per company leave policy,
and organization rules.

Step 4
Leave Approved

The authorized manager approves the leave request after reviewing the application, leave balance, and company policies. The request is now officially accepted.

Step 5
Leave Balance Updated

The system automatically deducts the approved leave days from the employee's available leave balance and updates the leave records.

Step 6
Notification sent

A confirmation notification is sent to the employee via email and the dashboard, informing them that the leave request has been successfully processed.
