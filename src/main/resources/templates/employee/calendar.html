Holiday Policy Notes
The holiday calendar is subject to change based on government notifications and organizational requirements.
  • National holidays are applicable to all employees.
  • Festival holidays may vary according to company policy and regional offices.
  • Employees required to work on a declared holiday may receive a compensatory off as per company policy.
  • Weekend holidays are automatically reflected in the calendar and are not deducted from leave balance.
  • Any newly declared public holiday will be updated by the HR Administrator.
  • Employees are advised to verify holiday details before planning leave applications.
